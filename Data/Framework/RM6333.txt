International Healthcare Professional Recruitment and Associated Services
All public sector organisations can recruit healthcare professionals from overseas using a wide range of experienced international recruitment organisations.
This is the description of the International Healthcare Professional Recruitment and Associated Services agreement. 
TheNHS Workforce Alliancehas produced this agreement. The NHS Long Term Plan has set out its ambitions for the NHS over the next 10 years, identifying ethical and sustainable international recruitment as a workforce priority.
Job roles you can recruit for will include (but is not limited to):
nursing and midwifery including health visitors and dental nursing
personal social services including social workers and residential healthcare
administrative services including human resources and finance roles
allied health professionals including clinical psychology roles and physiotherapists
emergency services including ambulance workers
For more information about the different roles you can recruit,take a look at the national job profiles website.This list is not complete. Over time we will identify other healthcare roles and professionals.
