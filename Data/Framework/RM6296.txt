Occupational Health and Related Services DPS
Access to preventative services and treatments to support employee physical and mental health and well-being. This agreement is available to all approved UK public sector bodies, the wider public sector and third-sector organisations.
This is the description of the Occupational Health and Related Services DPS agreement. 
This agreement will give you access to high-quality occupational health, employee assistance programmes and eye care services, including but not limited to:
full service providers (covering all occupational health and employee assistance programmes)
occupational health services
employee assistance programmes
eyecare and Hearing Services
health surveillance services
health screening services
vaccinations
guidance and counselling services
rehabilitation services
The available services reflect the needs and accessibility requirements of the modern and diverse workforce.
This agreement uses a Dynamic Purchasing System (DPS) and offers a range of pre-qualified regional, national and overseas providers. You can filter providers by:
service provision
location
delivery method and data storage
contract value per annum
This DPS is available to use until 27 October 2028. This agreement complies with the Public Contracts Regulations (PCR) 2015.

These are the benefits of the Occupational Health and Related Services DPS agreement. 
competitive market rates
the DPS questionnaire removes the need for standard pre-qualification questions, saving buyers and suppliers time
Crown Commercial Service (CCS) compliance checks to make sure suppliers follow agreement terms
a future-proofed solution that can be continually updated to reflect changes in market demand and to support the addition of new products and suppliers at any time
greater choice of providers, both at a national and regional level
standard terms and conditions agreed at DPS level with suppliers to negate the need to create your own contract schedules
virtual appointments to save costs by reducing wait times and time away from work
all suppliers have signed up to the public service contract which includes terms that cover GDPR, prompt payment,carbon net zero (CNZ)andsocial value
flexibility of contract award: customers can choose between rapid award and standard award depending on their needs
