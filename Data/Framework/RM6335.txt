Digital & Legacy Application Services
Access suppliers who can provide IT digital and legacy application services (DALAS) and will support the roll out of future applications that are less dependent on legacy technologies.
This is the description of the Digital & Legacy Application Services agreement. 
Available to all central government departments and UK public sector bodies including:
local authorities
health
police
fire and rescue
education
devolved administrations
You will be able to access a range of IT digital services to help you develop and implement future applications that meet your needs.
It can also help you to move to an application support model that isn’t dependent on legacy (old or outdated) technologies. This move away from legacy applications will allow you to provide more cost effective and innovative services to your users inline with government digital strategy.

These are the benefits of the Digital & Legacy Application Services agreement. 
encourages suppliers to offer more competitive prices, quality and innovation
quicker procurements allows for reduced time scales
offers a range of pricing models allowing flexible call-off contracts
fewer suppliers on each lot reducing the amount of resource needed for evaluations
increased strategy alignment strengthening commitment and collaboration among buyers and suppliers
