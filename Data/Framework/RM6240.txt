Public Sector Legal Services
This framework has been established to provide a simple and compliant route to market for a range of legal services requirements primarily to the wider public sector. It covers a wide range of commonly needed areas of law, including contracts, dispute resolution and litigation, contracts and corporate law.
Comprising 3 lots the framework offers both general and more specific legal services.
This is the description of the Public Sector Legal Services agreement. 
Replacing RM3788 it provides a simple route to market for buying a wide range of legal services. These are split across 3 lots.
Some of the many legal specialisms available to access through this agreement includes:
employment
pensions
education law
health and safety
licencing law
immigration
property and construction
litigation/dispute resolution
social housing
